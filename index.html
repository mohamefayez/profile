Before we start

A few good
questions.

Clear expectations.
From the first conversation.

How do we get started?

Send a short brief with your idea, the features you need, and any references. We can then discuss the scope and next steps on WhatsApp.

How much does a project cost?

There is no fixed price list. Pricing and payment arrangements are discussed for each project after the scope is clear. The budget field is a starting point for that conversation.

How long will it take?

The timeline depends on the scope, content, and integrations. Share your preferred deadline so we can discuss a realistic delivery plan before work begins.

What about revisions and delivery?

The number of revision rounds, review stages, and final deliverables will be agreed for your project before work begins.

Is support included after launch?

Support, maintenance, hosting, and any ongoing costs are discussed separately according to the project. No standard support period has been set.
